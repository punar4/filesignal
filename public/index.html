Rules decide, every time

No AI guesses a number here. Each result comes from a written rule, and the reason is printed next to it, so a CA can check any line in seconds. Same input, same output.

Matching GSTR-2B

Pass 1 matches GSTIN + invoice number exactly. Pass 2 forgives formatting ("CP/0412/26-27" = "CP-412") when the amount agrees. Pass 3 finds the same invoice under a slightly different GSTIN (a typo in your vendor master). Leftovers are "not in 2B" or "not in books". Duplicates in books are caught first.

Why 2B is the limit

Since April 2026, GSTR-3B cannot claim more ITC than GSTR-2B shows. So anything not matched is shown as on hold, and the supplier gets an email.

TDS under the Income-tax Act, 2025

Non-salary TDS now sits in section 393(1). Books still say 194C or 194J, so both are shown. Thresholds for tax year 2026-27: contracts ₹30,000 per bill or ₹1 lakh a year (1% individual/HUF, 2% others, manpower included); professional fees 10% and technical fees 2% above ₹50,000; commission 2% above ₹20,000; rent 10% (building) or 2% (machinery) above ₹50,000 a month; goods 0.1% above ₹50 lakh per seller if your turnover crossed ₹10 crore. No valid PAN: 20%.

Section from the ledger

When a line has no TDS section, it is read from the ledger name ("Legal fees" → professional). If the booked section disagrees with the ledger ("Consultancy" booked as 194C), both are computed and the higher one is shown, flagged for review.

Interest and disallowance

Interest for not deducting is 1% a month or part, from the date TDS became due; for a yearly threshold, that is the date the year's total crossed it. If tax is not deducted at all, 30% of the expense can be disallowed when computing income, so that figure is shown as a risk.

Your data is not kept

Files are read in memory to produce this check and the Excel report, then discarded. Nothing is written to disk or a database, and file contents are never logged.

What to check yourself

Payment codes for Form 140 are not filled in: published mappings disagree, so pick them from the return utility. QRMP (quarterly) filers have different GST dates. This is a working aid, not tax advice.
